Our project focuses on predicting HVAC runtime to better understand key drivers of energy consumption. The dataset consists of two distinct sources: event-driven indoor logs capturing system behavior and regularly sampled outdoor environmental data. Integrating these sources required careful alignment due to differences in timing and structure. We evaluated multiple modeling approaches, including rolling window techniques to capture temporal dependencies, as well as tree-based methods such as XGBoost and LightGBM. These approaches were chosen to balance interpretability and predictive performance while accounting for nonlinear relationships in the data.
